I will perform two new sample-based sound improvisations at the 'Bunker Down' event in Edinburgh on Feb 7, on invitation from the event curators Laura Leuzzi and Joe DeLappe. The event takes place in the Cold War era Barnton Bunker. Recordings will be available here after the live event.

Sketch for a Meditation on Coltrane, 2026, improvised electronic sound

Bunker Resonance Study, 2026, improvised electronic sound

Event promotion info:

"Come along to a unique, engaging, free event showcasing the work of a dynamic group of Scotland-based media artists within the intriguing space of Edinburgh’s secret nuclear bunker at Barnton Quarry. Prepare for an immersive encounter exploring spaces built for secrecy, protection, and survival in the event of nuclear war. We are repurposing these atmospheric corridors and rooms on the ground floor level, installing artworks and featuring live performances which elevate the experience, provoke thought and stimulate conversations, through technology.

The event features artists working in many different types of media, including film, video art, music, sound art, performance, interactive body augmentations, virtual reality, video games and photography. Their work is gathered within the overall theme of life in our uncertain, contemporary times – is it now time to Bunker Down?

Artists and performers featured will include: Emily Beaney, Biome Collective, John Butler, Joseph DeLappe, Zhiyang Ding, Gair Dunlop, Christine Ellison & Jules Rawlinson, Victoria Evans, Beverley Hood, Laura Leuzzi, Adam Lockhart, Hadi Mehrpouya, Christos Michalakos & Dimitris Papageorgiou, Emile Shemilt, Irvine & Spence, and Martin Zeilinger."
