A short video created in collaboration with Deniz Johns, for the exhibition 'Harun Farocki: Consider Labour' at Cooper Gallery, Dundee University in 2023. Contributing to Antje Ehmann and Harun Farocki's long-term project "Labour in a Single Shot," this work captures – in a single shot – the process of rendering an algorithmic artwork on a physical plotter as a line-drawing. The idea is to reflect on the outsourcing of artistic labour to machines.
