A short track created for the 2025 Listening Festival of the Dundee
Radio Club (aired in Dec 2025). The piece reworks sound material
recorded live in the late 1990s from a broken (?) broadcast on the
Austrian independent radio station Radio FRO (previously a pirate
station).

See the Dundee Radio Club website for more information.
